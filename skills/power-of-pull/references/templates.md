# Templates

Fill these in with the words of one real, named buyer. Mark every line as evidence (something they said or did) or assumption.

## PULL hypothesis

```
Based on:      [named real person, role, company]
Project:       [the to do item, in their words]
Unavoidable:   [the situation that made it top priority now]
List:          [options they are weighing, incl. DIY, hire, consultant, do nothing]
Limitations:   [why each option falls short, in their eyes]
Supply:        [what we offer that fits better than those options]
Weird not to buy?  [yes/no, one line why]
```

## Repeatable success story

"[Customer] needed to [project] because [situation]. They considered [options], but [limitations]. They chose [us] because we offered [supply]. As a result, they [results]."

Test: a prospect with the same PULL reads it and thinks "that's exactly me".

## Sales call outline

1. **Intro:** why did you take the call; what do you want from it; agree an agenda.
2. **Demand:** fill in PULL; describe what similar customers faced and ask what does *not* resonate; probe urgency and options; play their PULL back and wait for a clear yes.
3. **Supply:** three to five minutes on the part that fits their project; pause to check it matches their expectations; price as what you get, how others think about the price, what you pay.
4. **Next step:** on the calendar before the call ends.

## Debugging log

One page, updated after every batch of about five conversations:

* **Current state:** the PULL hypothesis and the factory numbers (pipeline, sales, delivery).
* **Ground truth log:** one line per conversation: name, their PULL, the supply they want, outcome.
* **This week's bottleneck and the one change made.**
